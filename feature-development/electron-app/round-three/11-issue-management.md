Now that we have the ability to mark issues as accepted, we need a view to be able to manage those issues. This should exist within the existing issues.tsx view and should be comprehensive. Issues that are marked as accepted should still show up in the results for all tables showing issues across the application, but there should be a filter in place to exclude them. If they are not excluded, they are grayed out or have an alternative appearance to indicate clearly that they have been accepted and are not a concern. Upon going to the issues.tsx view, there should be a place where users can manage issues that have specifically been accepted. I would expect this to be more than a trivial place to unaccept them. The onus is on the product manager to identify what features would be particularly useful here in the management of accepted and unaccepted issues. Why does a user choose to accept an issue? What is the correct vernacular besides accepted? Is it excluded? Is it acknowledged? Is it bypassed? It may be worth considering that there are other variations of this change that allow users to micromanage these issues. It may also be a good opportunity to wire those into the todo list or even consider replacing the todo list entirely with, instead, a collection of different semantic groups of issues. For example:

- Issues may be excluded.
- Issues may be temporarily muted for a period of time, or they may be marked as spurious. I think the spurious option is also interesting because it presents an opportunity to potentially improve upon our algorithms. This leads into a broader question of an upcoming phase, which is centered around tracking. How do we track and report these sorts of things so that we can improve the application over time?
